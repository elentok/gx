# Rest screen mascot images: Codex prompts

Two images, one per Rest period kind. Each has two variants: a transparent one and a scene one.
The Mental health design picked "square scene, edges fade into the page", and the scene variants
shipped. The transparent prompts were never generated and are kept only as a fallback.

Attach `webui/public/gx-menu-mascot.png` as the style reference for every prompt.
Output size: 1024×1024. Save the PNGs as shown below, then downscale to 640×640 for the repo.

## Shared style line

Glossy 3D mascot render in the same style as the attached reference: a silver robot-goblin with
pointy ears, a glowing blue visor and a ring of small floating blue orbit dots. Soft rim lighting,
blue glow accents.

## Bedtime (asleep in pyjamas)

File: `webui/public/gx-rest-bedtime.png` (scene, shipped).

Transparent:

> Shared style line. Full body. The mascot sleeps curled up, wearing soft blue pyjamas and a
> droopy nightcap. Its visor is dimmed to a faint glow. Small "Zzz" letters float above. The orbit
> dots drift slowly and are dim. Transparent background, no ground, no shadow plane, centred with
> padding, 1024×1024.

Scene:

> Shared style line. Full body. The mascot sleeps curled up, wearing soft blue pyjamas and a
> droopy nightcap. Its visor is dimmed to a faint glow. Small "Zzz" letters float above. The orbit
> dots drift slowly and are dim. Cosy night scene: deep navy sky, a few stars, a soft crescent moon
> behind it, a small round cloud as a pillow. The edges of the image fade to near-black (#0a0d11)
> in a soft vignette so it blends into a dark page. 1024×1024.

## Break (meditating)

File: `webui/public/gx-rest-break.png` (scene, shipped).

Transparent:

> Shared style line. Full body. The mascot sits cross-legged in a meditation pose, hands on knees,
> eyes (visor) closed to a calm thin line, a gentle smile. The orbit dots float in a slow, even
> ring around it. Transparent background, centred with padding, 1024×1024.

Scene:

> Shared style line. Full body. The mascot sits cross-legged in a meditation pose, hands on knees,
> eyes (visor) closed to a calm thin line, a gentle smile. The orbit dots float in a slow, even
> ring around it. It sits on a smooth round stone in a calm teal-blue twilight, with soft glowing
> mist and a few fireflies. The edges fade to near-black (#0a0d11) in a soft vignette.
> 1024×1024.
